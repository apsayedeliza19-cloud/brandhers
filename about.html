About BrandHERS

Empowering Women-Led MSMEs

BrandHERS is an extension project of the Department of Technology Communication Management, College of Information Technology and Computing (CITC), USTP, in partnership with ORO-TIPC and DTI Misamis Oriental. It equips women entrepreneurs in Cagayan de Oro with hands-on skills in visual and video marketing.

Our Mission

To empower women-led micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs) in Cagayan de Oro by enhancing their digital marketing capabilities through hands-on training in cost-effective multimedia tools.
